Bringing Awareness through Systems for Humans Lab

Research

Sensing the Future: Bridging Sensors and Language Intelligence

Exploring the synergy between sensor data and large language models to create intelligent systems that understand and interpret the world through a fusion of modalities.

Funding: National Science Foundation

Acoustic Intelligence: Advancing Audio and Speech with AI

Advancing the field of audio processing and speech recognition through artificial intelligence, enhancing human-computer interaction and auditory scene understanding.

Funding: National Science Foundation

Tiny Power, Big Impact: ML for Low-Power Edge Innovation

Pioneering the development of efficient machine learning algorithms for ultra-low-power edge devices, enabling intelligent sensing in resource-constrained environments.

Funding: National Science Foundation

Mind Meets Machine: AI Transforming Behavioral Health

Leveraging artificial intelligence to revolutionize behavioral health, providing innovative tools for diagnosis, monitoring, and treatment to improve developmental and mental well-being.

Funding: National Science Foundation
